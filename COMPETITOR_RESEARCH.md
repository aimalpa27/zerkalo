# COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md

Research date: 2026-09-16. Prices are public list prices found on official product pages where available; taxes, payment processing, hardware and regional terms may differ.

## Executive conclusion

The market splits into three groups:

1. **Full restaurant OS/POS** — iiko, Poster, r_keeper, Quick Resto, Paloma365, Syrve, Toast, Square, Lightspeed.
2. **Direct-ordering / growth layer** — Owner.com, Popmenu, GloriaFood.
3. **QR order/pay layer** — sunday and QR modules inside POS ecosystems.

For the first 10–50 Plait restaurants, competing as a full inventory/accounting POS is strategically expensive. The stronger wedge is: **fast QR ordering + waiter + KDS + owner analytics + measurable rule-based upsell + Kazakhstan integrations**, while integrating with incumbent POS.

## UX/onboarding patterns worth adapting

- **sunday:** QR-to-value is immediate; order/pay flow is the product, not a side module.
- **Toast:** kitchen performance and operational handoff are first-class, not an afterthought.
- **Square:** transparent entry pricing and low-friction signup.
- **Quick Resto:** explicitly sells “launch in minutes” and bundles setup/training.
- **Paloma365:** Kazakhstan-specific payments/integrations and ability to run on common devices.
- **Owner/Popmenu:** sells revenue outcome, repeat orders and direct guest ownership rather than software features alone.

## Common complaints / friction themes to validate with user-review research

This first pass prioritized official sources. Before using complaints in product decisions, run a separate review pass across current G2/Capterra/App stores/Reddit because complaints change by version and region. Themes worth testing, not yet treated as facts here:
- complex setup and training in full POS systems;
- add-on pricing/hardware lock-in;
- integration fragility;
- support delays;
- feature overload for small venues;
- payment processor dependence.

## What Plait should copy conceptually, not visually

1. Toast: KDS operational clarity + prep-time metrics.
2. sunday: QR guest experience + fast demo.
3. Owner.com: revenue attribution story.
4. Square: simple plans and onboarding.
5. Paloma365: KZ payment/integration pragmatism.
6. iiko/Poster: menu/POS synchronization so restaurants do not duplicate work.
